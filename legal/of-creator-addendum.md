## PURPOSE

This Addendum applies to Creators who maintain an active presence on OnlyFans ("OF") and supplements the Mobileyes Talent Management Agreement v2.0 ("Principal Agreement"). Where this Addendum conflicts with the Principal Agreement, this Addendum takes precedence for matters relating to the Creator's OF presence and associated brand safety considerations.

---

## 1. SCOPE OF REPRESENTATION

### 1.1 What Mobileyes Represents

Mobileyes represents the Creator for **external brand campaigns** on platforms including but not limited to: TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, Kick, Twitter/X, Snapchat, and podcast integrations.

### 1.2 What Mobileyes Does NOT Represent

Mobileyes does NOT manage, advise on, take commission from, or bear responsibility for:

- Content published on the Creator's OnlyFans page
- OnlyFans subscription pricing, pay-per-view content, tips, or custom content
- Creator's direct relationship with OnlyFans (the platform)
- Any OnlyFans management agency the Creator may separately engage
- Promotion of the Creator's OF page (unless a brand specifically commissions this)

### 1.3 Commission Boundary

- **20% commission** applies to all external brand campaigns secured through Mobileyes
- **0% commission** on OnlyFans revenue (subscriptions, tips, PPV, customs, referrals)
- **0% commission** on Creator's self-generated brand deals not introduced by Mobileyes
- If a brand campaign requires the Creator to post on their OF page, the 20% commission applies to the fee for that campaign deliverable only

## 2. CONTENT SEPARATION

### 2.1 Separation Warranty

Creator warrants that they maintain a clear and enforceable separation between:

(a) **Campaign Profiles** — public-facing profiles on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Twitch, Kick, or other platforms where brand campaign content is delivered. These profiles must comply with each platform's community guidelines and be suitable for the brands Mobileyes represents.

(b) **Personal OF Page** — the Creator's OnlyFans account, the content of which is the Creator's sole responsibility and outside the scope of Mobileyes' management.

### 2.2 Cross-Posting Prohibition During Campaigns

During any active campaign window (from brief acceptance to 30 days post-delivery):

- Creator must NOT cross-post explicit content from their OF page to campaign profiles
- Creator must NOT reference explicit OF content in campaign deliverables unless the brief explicitly requires it
- Creator may reference their OF in their bio/link-in-bio at all times (this is expected and acceptable)

### 2.3 Profile Audit Right

Mobileyes reserves the right to audit the Creator's campaign profiles (NOT their OF page) at any time to confirm compliance with brand safety requirements. This audit is limited to publicly visible content on campaign platforms only.

## 3. BRAND SAFETY CLASSIFICATION

### 3.1 Tier Assignment

Upon execution of this Addendum, Creator will be classified into one of three Brand Safety Tiers based on their disclosed OF content:

| Tier | OF Content Type | Campaign Eligibility |
|------|----------------|---------------------|
| **A — SFW** | Fitness, cooking, music, BTS, tutorials, lifestyle — no nudity or sexual content | All brands. No restrictions. |
| **B — Suggestive** | Lingerie, swimwear, implied nudity, provocative but non-explicit | Fashion, beauty, dating apps, alcohol, gaming, wellness. Brand informed of OF presence. |
| **C — Explicit** | Adult/sexual content | Only brands who have signed an Explicit Creator Acknowledgement. Mobileyes informs brand prior to engagement. |

### 3.2 Tier Disclosure

Creator must accurately disclose the nature of their OF content at the time of signing this Addendum. Misrepresentation of tier (e.g., claiming Tier A when content is Tier C) constitutes a material breach.

### 3.3 Tier Changes

If Creator's OF content changes in a way that would affect their tier classification (e.g., moving from SFW to explicit), Creator must notify Mobileyes within 7 days. Failure to notify may result in termination of this Addendum.

### 3.4 Brand Disclosure Process

For Tier B and Tier C creators:
- Mobileyes will inform potential brand partners of the Creator's OF presence BEFORE confirming any campaign
- The brand must acknowledge this in writing (email confirmation is sufficient)
- If a brand declines based on OF presence, this is NOT a breach by Mobileyes and no fee is owed

## 4. INDEMNIFICATION

### 4.1 Creator Indemnifies Mobileyes

Creator agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Mobileyes Pty Ltd, its directors, employees, and agents from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable legal fees) arising from or relating to:

(a) Content published on the Creator's OnlyFans page, regardless of when published

(b) Any brand's decision to terminate a campaign due to discovery of the Creator's OF content, where the Creator failed to accurately disclose their tier classification

(c) Third-party claims related to the Creator's adult content, including but not limited to: defamation, copyright infringement, privacy violations, or obscenity claims

(d) Reputational damage to a brand partner where such damage arises from the Creator's non-campaign content and the Creator failed to maintain content separation per Section 2

### 4.2 Mobileyes Indemnifies Creator

Mobileyes agrees to indemnify Creator against claims arising from:

(a) Mobileyes presenting the Creator to a brand without disclosing the Creator's OF presence (where disclosure was required under Section 3.4)

(b) Mobileyes misrepresenting the Creator's tier classification to a brand

### 4.3 Limitation

This indemnification does NOT apply where:
- The brand was fully informed of the Creator's OF presence and proceeded regardless
- The claim arises from content within the campaign deliverables that was approved by both Mobileyes and the brand

## 6. TERMINATION

### 6.1 Addendum Termination

This Addendum may be terminated independently of the Principal Agreement if:

- Creator requests removal of OF-related representation
- Mobileyes determines the Creator's content poses unacceptable risk to its brand or other represented creators
- Creator repeatedly fails to maintain content separation during active campaigns

### 6.2 Effect on Principal Agreement

Termination of this Addendum does NOT terminate the Principal Agreement. The Creator may continue under standard representation for non-OF-related campaigns.

## 7. ADDITIONAL TERMS

### 7.1 Age Verification

Creator confirms they are 18 years of age or older and that all individuals appearing in their OF content are 18 years of age or older and have provided verifiable consent. Creator must maintain records of such verification as required by applicable law.

### 7.2 Platform Compliance

Creator warrants that their OnlyFans account complies with OnlyFans' Terms of Service. Any suspension or termination of the Creator's OF account does not affect this Addendum or the Principal Agreement.

### 7.3 Existing OF Management

If Creator has a separate management arrangement for their OnlyFans page (e.g., an OF management agency):
- Creator must disclose this to Mobileyes at signing
- That arrangement does not conflict with this Addendum (Mobileyes manages external brand campaigns only)
- If a conflict arises between the OF manager and Mobileyes regarding a brand campaign, this Addendum takes precedence for the brand campaign in question

### 7.4 No Exclusivity on OF

This Addendum grants Mobileyes no rights, exclusivity, or control over the Creator's OnlyFans page, content strategy, pricing, or subscriber relationships.

### 7.5 Privacy

Mobileyes will not publicly disclose a Creator's OF presence or tier classification without the Creator's written consent, except as required for brand disclosure under Section 3.4.

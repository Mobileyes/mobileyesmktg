## 2. SCOPE OF MANAGEMENT

### 2.1 Services Provided by Mobileyes

Mobileyes will provide the following talent management services:

(a) **Campaign Acquisition** — Sourcing, negotiating, and securing brand partnership opportunities via direct outreach, Fabulate pipeline partnerships, and inbound brand enquiries.

(b) **Rate Negotiation** — Negotiating service day rates, per-stream rates, per-video rates, and hybrid campaign fees on Talent's behalf.

(c) **Brief Management** — Receiving, interpreting, and communicating campaign briefs including talking points, deliverables, timelines, and brand guidelines.

(d) **Integration Verification** — Automated technology-verified completion of deliverables including:
- Live stream monitoring (Twitch, Kick, YouTube Live)
- Video upload detection (YouTube, TikTok)
- Attribution layer verification (UTM links, promo codes, pinned comments, transcripts)
- Screenshot capture of brand integrations as proof-of-delivery
- Transcript-based verification of brand mentions and talking points

(e) **Payment Processing** — Invoicing brands on Talent's behalf and remitting Talent's share within the payment terms specified in Section 5.

(f) **Performance Reporting** — Providing Talent with campaign analytics, engagement metrics, and attribution data via the Mobileyes platform.

### 2.2 Talent's Primary Platform and Content Types

This Agreement covers live video and streaming content including but not limited to:

- Live streams on Twitch, Kick, YouTube Live
- Pre-recorded video uploads on YouTube, TikTok
- Shorts, Reels, and short-form content
- Community posts, Discord integrations
- Multi-platform simulcasts

### 2.3 Fabulate Pipeline

Mobileyes receives campaign briefs via its partnership with Fabulate Pty Ltd. Talent acknowledges that campaigns sourced through the Fabulate pipeline are subject to the same terms as directly sourced campaigns.

## 3. COMMISSION

### 3.1 Commission Rate

Mobileyes shall retain a commission of **20% (twenty percent)** of the gross campaign fee negotiated for each campaign ("Commission").

### 3.2 Calculation

- **Gross Campaign Fee:** The total amount invoiced to the brand/client for Talent's services.
- **Talent's Share:** 80% of Gross Campaign Fee.
- **Mobileyes Commission:** 20% of Gross Campaign Fee.

### 3.3 Service Day Rates

For campaigns structured as service day rates (e.g., "$X per stream day"):

- Commission applies to the full day rate × number of service days.
- Overtime, bonuses, or performance kickers are also subject to the 20% commission.

### 3.4 Hybrid Campaigns

For campaigns combining live streams with VOD/short-form deliverables:

- Commission applies to the total campaign package value.
- Individual deliverable rates within a package are not separately commissioned.

### 3.5 Exclusions

Commission does NOT apply to:
- Direct brand deals that Talent secures independently without Mobileyes introduction or involvement.
- Merchandise revenue, donations, subscriptions, or platform-native monetisation (e.g., Twitch subs, YouTube AdSense).

## 4. CAMPAIGN ACCEPTANCE AND DELIVERABLES

### 4.1 Right to Decline

Talent retains the right to decline any campaign opportunity presented by Mobileyes. Declining a campaign does not constitute a breach of this Agreement.

### 4.2 Acceptance

Once Talent accepts a campaign (via written confirmation, email, or platform acknowledgement), Talent is committed to delivering the agreed-upon content within the specified timeline.

### 4.3 Deliverable Requirements

For each accepted campaign, Talent agrees to:

(a) Deliver content matching the campaign brief specifications (format, duration, talking points, timing).

(b) Include agreed attribution elements:
- UTM links in video descriptions or pinned comments
- Verbal brand mentions and talking points as specified
- Promo codes (verbal, on-screen, or in description as specified)
- Required hashtags or disclosures (#ad, #sponsored as legally required)

(c) Maintain content live for the agreed period (minimum 30 days unless otherwise specified).

(d) Not accept conflicting brand deals during exclusivity windows specified in individual campaign briefs.

### 4.4 Tech-Verified Completion

Mobileyes uses automated technology to verify deliverable completion:

- **Live Stream Monitoring:** System detects stream start/end, verifies brand integration occurred during the stream.
- **Video Detection:** System detects new uploads within 48 hours and scrapes attribution layer.
- **Transcript Verification:** Auto-captions are pulled and searched for brand mentions and talking points.
- **Screenshot Evidence:** System captures proof of the integration (thumbnail, overlay, or description screenshot).
- **View Count Logging:** Metrics captured at time of detection.

Completion is confirmed when the system detects the content AND verifies the attribution layer (UTM, promo code, or brand mention present). This triggers the approval workflow and subsequent payment timeline.

### 4.5 Disputed Verification

If Talent believes the automated system has incorrectly flagged a deliverable as incomplete:
- Talent may submit the content URL directly for manual review.
- Mobileyes will manually verify within 2 business days.
- If verified manually, payment timeline commences from the date of manual verification.

## 5. PAYMENT TERMS

### 5.1 Payment Timeline

Mobileyes will pay Talent's share within **4 business days** of verified content completion.

The timeline operates as follows:

1. **Day 0:** Content published by Talent.
2. **Day 0–1:** Automated verification system detects content, scrapes attribution, captures proof.
3. **Day 1–2:** Mobileyes reviews verification evidence and approves completion.
4. **Day 2–6:** Payment processed to Talent (within 4 business days of approval).

### 5.2 Payment Trigger

Payment is triggered by **tech-verified completion** — not by brand payment to Mobileyes. Mobileyes bears the credit risk. Talent is paid regardless of whether the brand has settled their invoice.

### 5.3 Payment Method

Payments are made via:
- Bank transfer (Australian BSB/Account)
- Airwallex (for international Talent)

Talent must provide valid banking details via the Mobileyes platform. If banking details are not on file at the time payment is due, payment will be held until details are provided and processed within 2 business days thereafter.

### 5.4 Currency

All payments are in **AUD (Australian Dollars)** unless otherwise agreed in writing for specific campaigns.

### 5.5 Late Payment

If Mobileyes fails to pay within 4 business days of approval:
- A late payment fee of 2% of the outstanding amount per week applies.
- Talent may suspend acceptance of new campaigns until outstanding payments are settled.

## 6. INTEGRATION TRACKING

### 6.1 Platform Monitoring

By entering this Agreement, Talent consents to Mobileyes monitoring their public content channels for the purpose of:

- Detecting new uploads and live streams relevant to active campaigns.
- Verifying delivery of campaign requirements (attribution, brand mentions, talking points).
- Capturing performance metrics (views, engagement, audience data) for campaign reporting.

### 6.2 Data Usage

Monitoring data is used exclusively for:
- Verifying campaign deliverable completion.
- Generating performance reports for brand clients.
- Building Talent's performance profile for future campaign matching.

Monitoring data is NOT shared with third parties except:
- Anonymised and aggregated in pitch materials.
- With the specific brand client for the relevant campaign only.

### 6.3 YouTube PubSubHubbub

For YouTube Talent, Mobileyes subscribes to upload notifications via YouTube's PubSubHubbub/WebSub system. This is a standard YouTube API integration that receives public data only.

### 6.4 Transcript Verification

Mobileyes pulls publicly available auto-generated captions/transcripts to verify brand mentions. Full transcripts are not stored — only the relevant excerpt (approximately 200 characters surrounding the brand mention) is retained as proof-of-delivery evidence.

## 7. TERM AND TERMINATION

### 7.1 Initial Term

This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial term of **12 months**.

### 7.2 Renewal

After the initial term, this Agreement automatically renews on a month-to-month basis unless either party provides 30 days written notice of termination.

### 7.3 Termination by Talent

Talent may terminate this Agreement at any time with **30 days written notice**, subject to:
- Completion of all accepted campaign deliverables currently in progress.
- Payment of Commission on any campaigns accepted prior to termination notice.

### 7.4 Termination by Mobileyes

Mobileyes may terminate this Agreement immediately if Talent:
- Fails to deliver accepted campaign commitments without reasonable cause.
- Engages in conduct that materially damages brand relationships or Mobileyes reputation.
- Breaches exclusivity terms of an active campaign.
- Provides false or misleading performance data.

### 7.5 Post-Termination Obligations

Upon termination:
- Commission remains payable on all campaigns accepted prior to termination.
- Campaigns in progress must be completed (or brand must be formally notified of cancellation by Mobileyes).
- Mobileyes ceases channel monitoring within 7 days of termination effective date.

## 8. BRAND CANCELLATION PROTECTION

### 8.1 Brand-Unfriendly Cancellation

If a brand cancels a campaign **after Talent has accepted** and **before content delivery**:

(a) If cancellation occurs more than 7 days before the scheduled delivery date:
- No fee is payable.

(b) If cancellation occurs within 7 days of scheduled delivery:
- Talent receives **50% of the agreed fee** as a cancellation fee.
- Mobileyes will invoice the brand for the cancellation fee. If the brand refuses payment, Mobileyes bears the cost.

(c) If cancellation occurs after Talent has commenced production (e.g., scheduled stream, begun filming):
- Talent receives **100% of the agreed fee**.
- Mobileyes will pursue full payment from the brand.

### 8.2 Talent Cancellation

If Talent cancels an accepted campaign:

(a) More than 7 days before delivery: No penalty, but repeated cancellations (3+ in a rolling 6-month period) may result in reduced campaign priority.

(b) Within 7 days of delivery without reasonable cause: Mobileyes may pass through any brand-imposed penalties to Talent.

### 12.1 Applicable Law

This Agreement and the data processing activities described herein comply with the **Privacy Act 1988 (Cth)** and the **Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)**.

### 12.2 Data Collection

Mobileyes collects and processes the following personal information in connection with this Agreement:

- Public content data (video titles, descriptions, transcripts, view counts, engagement metrics)
- Financial information (bank details for payment processing, fee records)

### 12.3 Purpose Limitation

Personal information is collected and used solely for:
- Campaign delivery verification and proof-of-delivery
- Payment processing and financial record-keeping

### 12.4 Data Retention

### 12.5 Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting Talent's personal information:
- Mobileyes will notify affected Talent within **72 hours** of becoming aware of the breach
- Notification will include the nature of the breach, information affected, and remedial actions taken

### 12.6 International Data

For Talent based outside Australia:
- EU/EEA Talent: GDPR rights apply including right to access, rectification, erasure, and data portability. Mobileyes processes data under legitimate interest (contract performance).
- All international Talent: data is processed and stored on Google Cloud (Firebase) infrastructure with servers in the US and Australia.

### 12.7 Access and Correction

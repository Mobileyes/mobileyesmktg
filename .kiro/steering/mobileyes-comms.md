## Brand Voice

<cite index="2-22,2-23,2-24,2-25">Founded by someone who has sat on both sides of the table — brand and creator, platform
and agency — Mobileyes communicates with earned authority, not borrowed credibility.

### Voice Sounds Like
- Direct, confident, specific — names, numbers, dates
- Warm but not casual — we know the creator's world
- Industry-fluent — CPI, UA, APAC, live ops, hybrid-casual
- Self-aware about the agency's size — we're lean and fast, not big and slow
- Opinionated — we have views on what works and what doesn't

### Voice NEVER Sounds Like
- Corporate buzzwords — "synergy", "leverage", "ecosystem play"
- Hype without substance — "the #1 gaming talent agency"
- Overly casual — no emoji-heavy posts, no "vibes" copy
- AI-generated generic marketing — "exciting opportunities await"
- Defensive about competitors — never name or disparage GMG, AnyMind

### Terminology Rules
- Always "creators" or "talent" — NEVER "influencers" in any comms
- Always "live streaming" or "live video" — not just "gaming"
- Always "campaigns" — not "deals" or "collabs"
- Always "admin@mobileyes.live" — single inbox

## Messaging Pillars

### Pillar 1: Performance over promise
Every claim is backed by a number or a name. "4-day payment" is a commitment with a
mechanism. "20 years in gaming" is Joel's career, not a tagline.

### Pillar 2: Creators are professionals, not inventory
We talk about creators the way a good sports agent talks about athletes — with respect
for their craft, their audience, and their commercial value.

### Pillar 3: Brands deserve attribution, not impressions
When we talk to brands, we talk about outcomes. CPI. Installs. Revenue. "Reach" is a
lazy metric. Mobileyes exists because brands were sold reach and got nothing measurable.

### Pillar 4: Live video is the future, not a niche
Twitch, TikTok Live, YouTube Live, Kick, Shopee Live — live streaming is where audiences
are moving. Mobileyes was built for this format, not retrofitted for it.

## Automation Rules

### All auto-generated comms MUST:
1. Use the voice rules above (direct, warm, industry-fluent)
2. Include specific data points where available (not generic claims)
3. Queue for Joel's approval before sending — NOTHING goes out without review
4. Use the correct signature variant (standard vs brief response)
5. Respect SLA timelines (flag if approaching deadline)
6. Never use "influencer" — always "creator" or "talent"
7. Never mention competitors by name
8. Always include admin@mobileyes.live as reply-to

### Outreach email generation rules:
- Reference the target's recent activity (launch, campaign, hire)
- Include one specific data point about their market/audience
- Keep under 150 words for cold outreach
- End with a clear, low-commitment CTA (chat, call, coffee)
- Subject line: specific, not clickbait (e.g. "APAC creators for [Game Name] launch")

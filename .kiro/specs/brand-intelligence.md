# Brand Intelligence

## Goal
Research brands, identify campaign windows, find decision makers, and time outreach for maximum conversion.

## Workflow
1. **Research** — Search brand name, pull agency relationships, campaign history
2. **Seasonality** — Analyze when they typically run campaigns (Q1-Q4 patterns)
3. **Timing** — Calculate optimal outreach window (2-3 months before campaign)
4. **Decision Makers** — Find marketing/UA team on LinkedIn
5. **Outreach** — Generate insight-powered message with download/revenue data
6. **Track** — Monitor response, follow-up, pipeline progression

## Data Sources
- Web search (brand + "influencer campaign")
- LinkedIn (marketing team, agency appointments)
- Sensor Tower (app downloads, revenue, ad spend)
- Facebook Ad Library (active campaigns)
- TikTok Creative Center (branded content)
- StreamCharts (active sponsorships)
- App Store / Google Play (new releases, updates)

## Annual Planning
- Track campaign timing per brand across the year
- Identify recurring patterns (seasonal, event-driven, release-driven)
- Build outreach calendar 2-3 months ahead of expected campaigns
- Flag new game launches as immediate outreach opportunities

## Success Criteria
- Brand research returns agency + campaign history within search
- Seasonality patterns detected from 3+ data points
- Outreach messages include specific market data (downloads, revenue)
- Annual calendar populated with 50+ brand opportunities

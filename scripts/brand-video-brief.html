Production Setup

CameraiPhone (4K, 24fps or 30fps). Lock focus manually per shot.
LightingTwo small lights: one key (angled, creates texture on metal/cables), one subtle fill. Dark background.
BackgroundDark/minimal. Black or navy backdrop. Reflector screen behind for controlled light. Keep it simple — the shots are all close-up so background barely shows.
Shooting methodEach shot filmed separately (5-10 sec each, trim to 1.5s in edit). Get 3-4 takes per shot.
AnglesBird's eye (looking down at hands) or 45° from side. Never straight-on — the angle makes gear look cinematic.
Edit softwareCapCut Desktop (free) or iMovie. Hard cuts only — no transitions, no effects.
💡 Pro tip: Shoot your hands from ABOVE (bird's eye) for shots 1, 3, 5. Shoot from the SIDE at 45° for shots 2, 4, 6. This variety in angle creates visual rhythm without needing camera movement.

Shot List — Detailed

6 shots. 1.5 seconds each. Hard cuts. Each shot is a self-contained action with a satisfying mechanical moment.

SHOT 1: XLR Cable Plug-in Hands plugging XLR cable into audio interface. Close-up. Shallow depth of field.
📷 Angle: Bird's eye (looking down). Focus on the connector meeting the port.
🔊 Sound: Cable click/snap as it seats. Slight cable rustle.
⏱ Duration: 1.5 seconds. Start with cable approaching, cut on the click.
SHOT 2: Tripod Legs Opening Tripod legs clicking/snapping open. Floor-level angle looking up at the mechanism.
📷 Angle: Low, 45° from side. Camera at floor level looking up at the legs spreading.
🔊 Sound: Mechanical snap/click of leg locks. Metal on metal.
⏱ Duration: 1.5 seconds. Start with one leg extending, cut after all lock.
SHOT 3: Phone Slotting into Mount Phone being placed into a mount/clamp. Satisfying click of it locking into position.
📷 Angle: Bird's eye (looking down) or slightly angled. Focus on the phone edge meeting the clamp.
🔊 Sound: Clamp mechanism clicking closed. Solid, secure.
⏱ Duration: 1.5 seconds. Phone enters frame, seats, clamp closes. Cut on the lock.
SHOT 4: Ring Light Switching On Finger flips switch — ring light blooms from dark. Light fills the frame.
📷 Angle: 45° from side. Show the ring light from slight angle so you see the shape. Dark before, illuminated after.
🔊 Sound: Subtle switch click, then silence as light fills (the visual IS the sound here).
⏱ Duration: 1.5 seconds. Dark → finger on switch → bloom of light. Cut at peak brightness.
SHOT 5: Shotgun Mic Mounting Shotgun microphone being slid into camera hot shoe and locked down.
📷 Angle: Bird's eye or close side angle. Focus on the metal shoe mechanism.
🔊 Sound: Metal sliding into mount, thumbscrew tightening. Precision.
⏱ Duration: 1.5 seconds. Mic slides in, locks. Cut on the tighten.
SHOT 6: THE RECORD BUTTON Index finger pressing RECORD. Red LED illuminates. THE moment. Hold.
📷 Angle: Close-up, 45° from side. Finger approaches button, presses, red light comes on. Slightly longer hold than other shots.
🔊 Sound: Button press (tactile click), then the classic RECORD BEEP. Then silence.
⏱ Duration: 2 seconds. Finger approaches (0.5s) → press + beep (0.5s) → red light holds (1s). This is the payoff moment.
⚠️ Shot 6 is the hero moment. Everything builds to this. The red LED coming on IS the brand. Give it an extra half-second of hold time. The audience should feel the stillness after the beep — that's when you're LIVE.

Edit Workflow

Step 1Import all clips into CapCut Desktop (or iMovie)
Step 2Select best take of each shot (sharpest focus, best timing)
Step 3Trim each to ~1.5 seconds (Shot 6 gets 2 seconds)
Step 4Arrange in order: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5 → 6 → End Frame
Step 5Hard cuts between each. NO transitions, no fades between shots.
Step 6Add ambient audio from original recordings (cable clicks etc.)
Step 7Add RECORD beep on Shot 6 (search "record button beep" on Freesound.org)
Step 8Create end frame (navy solid + text overlays with fade-in)
Step 9Colour grade: cool shadows, warm highlights, desaturate slightly EXCEPT red
Step 10Export: 1080p MP4 for web. Also export 9:16 vertical crop for TikTok/Reels.
⚠️ Do NOT use: AI video generators, stock footage, animated graphics, background music, face reveals, transitions between shots, or any green (#1DB954) anywhere.

1. Scope of Representation

  1. The Agency will represent the Creator for the purpose of securing, negotiating, and managing brand campaign opportunities across live streaming, video content, and social media platforms.
  2. Representation is non-exclusive unless otherwise agreed in writing. The Creator may accept campaigns independently, but campaigns secured by the Agency are subject to the commission terms below.
  3. The Agency will match the Creator with briefs that align with their audience, content style, and brand safety profile. The Creator has the right to decline any brief.

2. Commission & Payment

  1. The Agency will charge a commission of 20% of the total campaign fee payable to the Creator for all campaigns secured by the Agency.
  2. The Creator will be paid within four (4) business days of content approval by the brand. This is the Agency's baseline standard — not a conditional perk.
  3. The Agency carries the float: the Creator is paid regardless of whether the brand has paid the Agency's invoice at that time.
  4. All fees quoted to the Creator are the Creator's net fee (after commission deduction). The Agency invoices the brand for the gross amount (Creator fee + commission + service fee).
  5. Payment will be made via bank transfer to the Creator's nominated account.
Payment example: Brand pays $10,000 total. Agency commission (20%) = $2,000. Creator receives $8,000 within 4 business days of content approval.

3. Brief Acceptance & Content Delivery

  1. The Agency will present campaign briefs to the Creator via email. The Creator has 48 hours to accept or decline a brief unless otherwise specified.
  2. Upon acceptance, the Creator agrees to deliver content in accordance with the brief requirements, deliverables, and timeline specified.
  3. Content must include the agreed integration (brand mention, product placement, link, promo code, or other deliverable as specified in the brief).
  4. The Creator retains full creative control over their content style, voice, and presentation. The Agency will never provide fully scripted copy — only a brief with brand truth and key messages.

4. Integration Verification

  1. The Creator must provide proof of integration delivery: a timestamped screenshot, clip, or link showing the brand integration within their published content.
  2. Integration verification triggers the 4-day payment clock. No verification = no payment processing until proof is provided.
  3. The Agency may use platform APIs to automatically verify content delivery where available (YouTube, Twitch, Kick, TikTok).

5. Usage Rights

  1. Usage rights for content produced under Agency-secured campaigns are as specified in each individual campaign brief.
  2. Unless otherwise specified, usage is limited to the brand's own channels for the duration specified in the brief (typically 3-12 months).
  3. Extended usage, repurposing, or usage beyond the agreed scope requires additional negotiation and fees.
  4. The Creator retains ownership of their content. The brand receives a license to use as specified — not ownership.

6. Brand Safety & Restrictions

  1. The Creator will not knowingly engage in conduct that would damage the brand's reputation during an active campaign period.
  2. The Agency reserves the right to decline matching the Creator with campaigns that present brand safety concerns for either party.
  3. The Creator must comply with all applicable advertising disclosure requirements (AANA Code, FTC where applicable) including #ad or #sponsored disclosure.

7. Exclusivity

  1. This representation is non-exclusive. The Creator may work with other agencies, managers, or accept direct brand deals.
  2. Campaign-level exclusivity (e.g. "no competing brand content during campaign period") may be specified in individual briefs and will be compensated with an additional fee.
  3. It is the Creator's responsibility to disclose any existing exclusivity arrangements that may conflict with Agency-secured campaigns.

8. Term & Termination

  1. This agreement has no fixed term and may be terminated by either party with 14 days written notice to admin@mobileyes.live.
  2. Campaigns accepted prior to termination will be fulfilled under these terms regardless of termination.
  3. Outstanding payments owed to the Creator will be paid within 4 business days of content approval, regardless of termination status.

9. Independent Contractor

  1. The Creator is an independent contractor and is not at any time an employee of the Agency.
  2. The Creator is responsible for their own tax obligations including GST registration (if applicable), income tax, and superannuation.
  3. The Agency will not make PAYG withholding deductions from Creator payments unless required by law and agreed in writing.

10. Governing Law

  1. This agreement is governed by the laws of New South Wales, Australia and the parties submit to the non-exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of NSW and the Commonwealth of Australia.
  2. Services may be performed internationally (APAC region). The governing law remains NSW regardless of where services are performed.

11. General

  1. The Agency will exercise all rights under this agreement in a manner consistent with the Creator's good name, reputation, and commercial interests.
  2. Where there is any inconsistency between this agreement and an individual campaign brief, the campaign brief terms will take precedence for that campaign only.
  3. Any amendments to this agreement must be in writing and agreed by both parties.
